Nurse line cost-savings analysis

A health plan wanted to know if its nurse advice line saved money. The call system and the claims system share no member ID, so I linked them with regex cleaning and the matching passes above, grouped claims into conditions and treatments in SQL, and priced every change in treatment plan.

CIS 412, Fall 2025 Machine learning

Flight delay prediction

Classified 2,201 DC-to-New York flights as on time or delayed. I compared a decision tree with logistic regression, then rebuilt the model using only what's known before departure to check how much of the accuracy came from data leakage.

0.898test accuracy for logistic regression, vs. 0.848 for the decision tree
